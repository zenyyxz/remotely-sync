Remotely Sync

Remotely Sync is a free, open-source community plugin for Obsidian. It helps you synchronize notes and other vault files between your devices using cloud storage accounts that you choose and configure.

The plugin supports Google Drive and several other cloud and self-hosted storage providers. Your files are synchronized between your Obsidian device and your selected provider. Remotely Sync is independent of Obsidian and is not the official Obsidian Sync service.

Google Drive authorization uses this domain only to complete OAuth authorization and token exchange. This website does not provide storage for your vault files.
