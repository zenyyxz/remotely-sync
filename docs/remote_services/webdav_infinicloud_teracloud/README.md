# InfiniCLOUD WebDAV (formerly TeraCLOUD)

## Get your WebDAV details

Register an account, then open <https://infini-cloud.net/en/modules/mypage/usage/>. In **Apps Connection**, enable **Turn on Apps Connection**. This page provides the address, account, and WebDAV password; the WebDAV password is different from your account password.

![](./infinicloud_account.png)

## Enter the connection settings

Select WebDAV in Remotely Sync settings and enter the address, account, and **WebDAV password** rather than your account password.

![](./infinicloud_rs_setting.png)

## Verify and sync

Click **Check Connectivity** in settings. After the connection is confirmed, sync.
